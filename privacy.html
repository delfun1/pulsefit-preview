Who we are

PulseFit is operated by [not set], [not set] ("we"). Contact for privacy questions: [not set].

What we collect

  • Account: name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), language and time zone.
  • Profile you choose to add: photo, phone number, city, date of birth, fitness goals, experience, available equipment and any limitations you decide to share.
  • Health and fitness data you record: workouts, measurements (for example weight), personal records and progress photos. You decide whether your coach can see measurements and photos.
  • Coaching: bookings, session notes and reviews. Coaches also provide their certifications.
  • Messages: chat messages and photos you send to your coach or client.
  • Store: orders, delivery addresses and wishlists.
  • Payments: we record the amount, currency, status and reference of each payment. Card and Mobile Money details are entered on our payment provider's page (Flutterwave). We never receive or store card numbers or PINs.
  • AI assistant: the questions you ask and the answers.
  • Devices: a push-notification token for each device where you allow notifications.

We do not use advertising trackers or sell personal data.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service you asked for: your account, workout plans, bookings, the store, payments, messages and notifications.
  • To keep the service safe: preventing fraud and abuse, and moderating reported messages.
  • To meet legal duties, such as keeping accounting records of payments and orders.

Service providers that process data for us

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage (data stored in the [not set] region).
  • Flutterwave: payments.
  • Anthropic: the AI assistant. Your question and the relevant parts of your profile and recorded activity are sent to generate an answer.
  • Resend: emails.
  • Google Firebase: push notifications.

Who can see your data

You, plus your coach for the information you share with them. Our administrators can see account, subscription, order, booking and payment records to run the service. They cannot see your health data or messages from the admin dashboard, except messages someone has reported for moderation. Every administrator action is recorded in an audit log.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account, your personal data and files are removed. Payment, invoice, refund and order records are kept without your name for as long as accounting law requires; orders keep the delivery address printed on them.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: in the app, Profile → Export my data.
  • Correct it: Profile → Edit profile.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete my account (details).
  • Notifications: Profile → Notification settings.
  • For any other request, write to [not set].

Age

The AI assistant is available from age 16. PulseFit isn't intended for children without a parent's or guardian's involvement.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Access is limited by rules enforced in the database itself. Administrators use two-factor sign-in.

Changes

We'll tell you in the app before important changes take effect.
