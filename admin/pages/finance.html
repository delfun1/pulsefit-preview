How these numbers are calculated. Revenue counts only confirmed money (succeeded, partially refunded or refunded payments), by payment date. Pending, failed, cancelled and disputed payments are never counted as revenue — they are listed separately below. Each currency is reported on its own; nothing is converted. Net = gross − refunds − provider fees. Platform commission = commission % × coaching money kept after refunds.
