Everything between you and your next personal record

Four parts of one app, all signed in with the same account.

  • Workouts

    Follow a plan or build your own, then log every set as you go.

    • Sets, reps and weight, with your personal records kept for you
    • Progress charts, body measurements and progress photos
    • Your measurements and photos stay private unless you share them with your coach

  • Coaches

    Train with certified coaches, by video or in person.

    • See each coach's certifications and reviews from their clients
    • Message your coach and send photos in the app
    • Reminders a day and an hour before every session

  • Store

    Equipment, clothing and supplements, delivered to your door.

    • Pay with Mobile Money or card
    • Members with a plan get a discount on store prices
    • Follow your order from payment to delivery

  • AI assistant

    Ask about your training and get answers built from your own records.

    • It only reads what you've logged: workouts, measurements and goals
    • It gives fitness guidance, never medical diagnoses
    • Available from age 16; your plan sets how many questions you can ask each day
