Delete your PulseFit account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open PulseFit and sign in.
  2. On Home, tap the profile icon at the top (on a tablet or computer: Profile in the side menu).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Can't sign in?

Email [not set] from the address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm the request with you and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profile and sign-in, photos, workouts, measurements, progress photos, messages, AI conversations, addresses, cart, wishlist, reviews, devices and notifications. Upcoming sessions are cancelled and your coach is told.

What is kept

Records of payments, invoices, refunds, orders and past coaching sessions, without your name or account, because accounting law requires us to keep them. Orders keep the delivery address printed on them.

When deletion has to wait

If an order you paid for is still on its way, or a payment is still being processed, the app asks you to try again later.
